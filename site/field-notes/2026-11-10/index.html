Preethi Kasireddy @iam_preethi · Nov 9

"My husband's semen analysis came back normal"

Okay but did anyone check his homocysteine?

A semen analysis counts his sperm. It can't see whether their DNA is damaged. In a study with 5,032 men, those with the highest homocysteine had 2x the odds of damaged sperm DNA, 20% vs 11%.

If you're trying to conceive, ask for a sperm DNA fragmentation test. Under 15% is ideal, and 15 to 25% is fair. And get his homocysteine checked. It's a cheap blood test, and high levels often mean he's low on folate, B12, or B6.
