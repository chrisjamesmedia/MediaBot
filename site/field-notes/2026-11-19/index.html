Nature & History

J.K. Lundblad | Teaching Progress @JK_Lundblad · Nov 18

ApoStructura is correct.

One of my favorite essay on this subject is on R&P.

I charted the progress in transport speeds here:

It should be noted that the breakthrough in transport come in pulses; as one mode begins to decline, the other rises.

Also worth mentioning that the total size of the network grows larger than the last, opening more corners of the world.

Also, my favorite fact: the faster the transportation mode, the higher the value items transported.

Ships and rail, for instance, move raw materials, trucks move intermediate goods, and aircraft high value consumer goods.
