Paul Millerd @p_millerd · Nov 18

Ive probably talked to over 1000 people who are contemplating leaving their job or making a change.

There’s basically two camps
1. They must leave. They just want friends, advice, or ideas to make it smoother.

2. The stuck but afraid: don’t like what they’re doing now but have incredibly detailed pictures and stories about what could go wrong

Almost everyone in camp one makes some sort of leap. They rarely “go back”

the majority of camp two don’t make a major change and if they do probably half of those return to their previous positions
