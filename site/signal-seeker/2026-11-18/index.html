Front Page

Nvidia's board says it will not bring Jensen Huang back, on a blowout earnings night

On the earnings call, interim CEO Colette Kress said Huang resigned, the board accepted it and "will not reinstate Jensen," per @firesidealpha. Hours later Semafor reported a board faction is still pushing for his return, and that Huang has spoken with at least one director about it, @KobeissiLetter noted. Shopify's Tobi Lütke, quoted by @davidsenra, argued there is no succession for a founder like him.

X @firesidealpha · X @KobeissiLetter · X @davidsenra

Nvidia's numbers: "demand far exceeds supply"

Revenue grew 103% from a year ago and EPS 106%, with a $380B buyback, @MrMikeInvesting summarized. @aleabitoreddit pulled the industry read-through: supply commitments jumped from $279B to $390B, mostly multi-year memory procurement; Nvidia guided to about 75% growth; it put the industry cloud backlog at $2.5 trillion, up from $2 trillion, with Hopper rentals at 2x original pricing; and co-packaged optics switches are in volume production.

X @MrMikeInvesting · X @aleabitoreddit · X @babyfolio

Nvidia says it will keep backing neoclouds, more selectively

Nvidia called neoclouds "an increasingly important part of the global AI infrastructure ecosystem" but said it will be "disciplined in how we commit capital," @ren_stocks quoted. @daniel_koss read it as Nvidia starting to pick winners, with Nebius and CoreWeave the obvious ones; @babyfolio noted the call did not announce a blanket stop to neocloud financing.

Four Cards

If a parcel is marked Express, it has a red sticker.

StandardRed stickerBlue stickerExpress

Each card has one thing on each side. Which cards must you turn over to check the rule?

Show answer

Turn Express and Blue sticker. Express could hide something that breaks the rule; Blue sticker breaks it if the other side turns out to be Express. Red sticker can't break it (the rule never says only those cards have it), and Standard isn't covered by the rule at all. Most people pick the card that matches the rule's second half instead of the one that could falsify it.
