Nvidia’s board opens an independent probe into Jensen Huang; the stock slides premarket

Huang told employees he had been unfaithful to his wife, after reporting tied him to a businesswoman US authorities reportedly concluded was a Chinese intelligence asset. Nvidia’s board said it has “no reason to believe” national security or company secrets were compromised and put independent directors and outside counsel in charge of an investigation; Huang said he will cooperate fully. @GavinSBaker, who has known him for almost 30 years, said he does not believe Huang would knowingly hurt America or Nvidia. US data center construction spending up 90% a year, a record $96.5B rate

September data center construction ran at a record annualized $96.5 billion, up 90% from a year earlier and the biggest jump in ten years, @KobeissiLetter reported; it has nearly quadrupled since the start of 2024. Office construction fell 8% to $46.0 billion, near its lowest since 2016. Separately, @jukan05 relayed a Nikkei report that Musk’s Terafab told equipment suppliers it is targeting 1 million wafers a month from 2032, 700,000 of them for memory.

Musk: the Cybertruck line shuts in about eight months to make room for Cybercab

Elon Musk said Cybertruck orders far exceed production, and Tesla will build as many as it can before shutting the line in about eight months, returning many deposits. He said the truck is not unprofitable; the complexity simply costs more than the extra Cybercab output it would free up.

Mental Math

  1. 16 × 6 = ?
  2. 65% of 460 = ?
  3. Fermi: you read 200 words a minute for 30 minutes a day. How many days for a 60,000-word book?
Show answer
  1. 96 16 × 6 = 10 × 6 + 6 × 6 = 96
  2. 299 10% is 46, so 65% = 6 × 46 + half of 46 = 299
  3. 10 days 200 × 30 = 6,000 words a day; 60,000 ÷ 6,000 = 10.0, so 10 days

Four Cards

If a day's forecast says rain, the card shows an umbrella on the back.

RainSunnyUmbrellaScarf

Each card has one thing on each side. Which cards must you turn over to check the rule?

Show answer

Turn Rain and Scarf. Rain could hide something that breaks the rule; Scarf breaks it if the other side turns out to be Rain. Umbrella can't break it (the rule never says only those cards have it), and Sunny isn't covered by the rule at all. Most people pick the card that matches the rule's second half instead of the one that could falsify it.
