Front Page

CoreWeave: customers now prepay up to 100%, and demand runs 10x capacity

CoreWeave's Q3 call said customers are now funding up to 100% of contract value upfront, with qualified demand about ten times available capacity through 2027 and 2028 interest already several times what it has. @aleabitoreddit added that older Ampere and Hopper rental prices rose 30-50% quarter over quarter; @GavinSBaker says the shift from offtake financing to prepayments makes operating cash flow to EBITDA the metric that matters and shortens GPU payback. The stock rose more than 16% overnight.

X @mvcinvesting · X @aleabitoreddit · X @GavinSBaker · X @KobeissiLetter

TSMC calls leading-node demand "insatiable"

Quoting TSMC at the Nikkei Forum, @aleabitoreddit reports the company sees no sign of an AI slowdown, says demand is still far above what it can supply, and names power and substrates as customers' bottlenecks. On its mid-to-high-50s growth estimate for AI accelerator revenue through 2029, C.C. Wei said it "probably will be higher."

X @aleabitoreddit

Nvidia erases Tuesday's drop amid the Huang story

@KobeissiLetter reports Nvidia rose more than 6% as investors bought the dip, erasing the prior day's decline tied to the Jensen Huang reporting, while Nasdaq 100 futures climbed about 1.5% on CoreWeave's results.

X @KobeissiLetter · X @KobeissiLetter

SpaceX is building Kessel, a custom Vera Rubin rack for Earth and orbit

@SemiAnalysis_ says SpaceX is developing Kessel, a custom Vera Rubin NVL72 rack that swaps the internal NVLink cable cartridges for a passive PCB midplane, keeping 72 GPU packages, 18 compute trays and 9 switch trays arranged back-to-back. It is designed for both terrestrial and orbital deployment; the open question is whether the smaller design makes a midplane practical.

Four Cards

If a card is red on one side, it has a circle on the other.

RedCircleTriangleGreen

Each card has one thing on each side. Which cards must you turn over to check the rule?

Show answer

Turn Red and Triangle. Red could hide something that breaks the rule; Triangle breaks it if the other side turns out to be Red. Circle can't break it (the rule never says only those cards have it), and Green isn't covered by the rule at all. Most people pick the card that matches the rule's second half instead of the one that could falsify it.
