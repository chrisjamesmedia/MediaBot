Researcher: Chinese labs' newest models were trained on export-barred Blackwell chips

@willdepue says model weights carry a fingerprint of the GPU that initialized them, and that after searching more than six trillion chip-layout and seed combinations he matched six Chinese models, including DeepSeek-V4-Pro, Kimi K3 and MiniMax-M3, to Nvidia Blackwell systems, four to Grace Blackwell racks. He says a B300 reproduced DeepSeek-V4-Flash's starting weights bit for bit while an H100 matched none, and estimates the largest runs used 2,000-3,000 Blackwell GPUs each. He says researchers at OpenAI and Anthropic independently verified the result.

Mental Math

  1. 25 × 4 = ?
  2. 85% of 160 = ?
  3. Fermi: you read 300 words a minute for 15 minutes a day. How many days for a 60,000-word book?
Show answer
  1. 100 25 × 4 = 20 × 4 + 5 × 4 = 100
  2. 136 10% is 16, so 85% = 8 × 16 + half of 16 = 136
  3. 14 days 300 × 15 = 4,500 words a day; 60,000 ÷ 4,500 = 13.3, so 14 days

Four Cards

If a parcel is marked Express, it has a red sticker.

Red stickerExpressStandardNo sticker

Each card has one thing on each side. Which cards must you turn over to check the rule?

Show answer

Turn Express and No sticker. Express could hide something that breaks the rule; No sticker breaks it if the other side turns out to be Express. Red sticker can't break it (the rule never says only those cards have it), and Standard isn't covered by the rule at all. Most people pick the card that matches the rule's second half instead of the one that could falsify it.
