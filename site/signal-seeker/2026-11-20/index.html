Four Cards

If a card has a vowel on one side, it has an even number on the other.

74RU

Each card has one thing on each side. Which cards must you turn over to check the rule?

Show answer

Turn U and 7. U could hide something that breaks the rule; 7 breaks it if the other side turns out to be U. 4 can't break it (the rule never says only those cards have it), and R isn't covered by the rule at all. Most people pick the card that matches the rule's second half instead of the one that could falsify it.
