The AI trade splits: hyperscalers rally while neoclouds sell off

On Wednesday, Amazon and Microsoft each jumped about 7% and Google about 4%, while CoreWeave fell nearly 10%, IREN about 11%, and Nebius about 6–7%. Commentators on X read the move as a bet that data-center pushback and local delays will hit pure-play neoclouds harder than the big cloud platforms, consolidating the market toward the hyperscalers. Separately, Oguz Erkan argued semiconductor valuations have already peaked relative to margins, with the easy money in chips largely made and demand expected to ease after 2027.

Semis cycle

OEOguz Erkan@oguzerkan · Nov 5

We are likely near the peak of the AI cycle. Historically, semiconductor valuations peak just before margins peak, which indicates a turning point in the cycle. Forward multiples have been contracting this year while margins have kept expanding. Given that multiples roll over before margins, we’ll likely see peak margins next year. This means demand is expected to ease post-2027. So, the easy money in semiconductors has already been made. There could still be some upside to $NVDA and $AMD, depending on how fast demand cools post-2027, but nobody should expect the violent upward moves we saw in the last 2–3 years.

Four Cards

If a card has a vowel on one side, it has an even number on the other.

R29A

Each card has one thing on each side. Which cards must you turn over to check the rule?

Show answer

Turn A and 9. A could hide something that breaks the rule; 9 breaks it if the other side turns out to be A. 2 can't break it (the rule never says only those cards have it), and R isn't covered by the rule at all. Most people pick the card that matches the rule's second half instead of the one that could falsify it.
