DDaniel@danielisdizzy · Oct 31

$AMD will generate more than $100B in annual revenue from server CPUs alone by 2030. Lisa Su expects the server CPU TAM to reach $220B by 2030, with AMD capturing more than 50% of that market. And I wouldn’t be surprised if the opportunity turns out to be even bigger. Just look at how aggressively AMD has raised its server CPU TAM projections for 2030: • November 2025: $60B • Q1 2026: $120B • October 2026: $220B $60B → $120B → $220B. 50% of $220B = $110B in annual revenue for AMD. From CPUs alone. And that doesn’t even include the massive opportunity in GPUs. For context, AMD generated approximately $41B in total revenue over the last 12 months. The explosion of AI agents will unleash an unprecedented surge in CPU demand. AI agents don’t just generate responses. They execute code, interact with applications, process data, and coordinate complex workflows. As billions of AI agents begin operating autonomously, server CPU demand will explode. $AMD will be one of the biggest beneficiaries of the agentic AI revolution.
