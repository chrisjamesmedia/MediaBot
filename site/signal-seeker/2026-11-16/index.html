Kuo: OpenAI and Anthropic TPU orders push Google's shipment visibility to 2029

Per analyst Ming-Chi Kuo, quoted by @jukan05, combined 2028-2029 demand is about 12M TPU units for OpenAI (designed by MediaTek) and about 14M for Anthropic (designed by Broadcom). Kuo puts realistic shipments at 8-9M, 14-15M and 16-18M units for 2027-2029, still growing in 2029 even though the market broadly expects AI capex to peak in 2028.

Musk: Starship V4 grows about 40% longer, with nine engines

Starship V4 will be about 70 m long, roughly a 40% length increase that allows more than 60% more propellant, @elonmusk posted. Engine count rises to nine, adding three more Raptor-Vacuum engines, all higher-thrust V4 engines.

Yet investors have convinced themselves that neoclouds are heading toward a financing crisis. $NBIS, $CRWV, and $IREN have all lost more than 50% of their market capitalizations from their peaks. The market is treating a financing crisis as inevitable while these companies continue securing billions in capital. Investors are dumping some of the fastest-growing companies in the world because they don’t understand how their expansion is financed. This is an enormous opportunity. And I’m buying.

Four Cards

If a card is red on one side, it has a circle on the other.

BlueTriangleRedCircle

Each card has one thing on each side. Which cards must you turn over to check the rule?

Show answer

Turn Red and Triangle. Red could hide something that breaks the rule; Triangle breaks it if the other side turns out to be Red. Circle can't break it (the rule never says only those cards have it), and Blue isn't covered by the rule at all. Most people pick the card that matches the rule's second half instead of the one that could falsify it.
